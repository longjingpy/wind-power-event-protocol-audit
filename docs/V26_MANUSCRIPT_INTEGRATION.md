## Scientific question and argument

When do complete power trajectories recover more independent physical information
than strong scalar descriptions? The new Results 2.6 identifies a complexity-dependent
benefit: compound power events show substantial full-wind-process gains at both Hill
instruments, and all three reference populations show improved internal geometry.
The existing polarity experiments supply the information-loss mechanism; the new
trajectory task supplies a broader physical test. Economic findings remain unchanged.
