## Integrated contribution

Ordered, scale-restored trajectories have their strongest incremental value in
power-defined compound events. Against validation-selected strong scalar controls
using identical native support, full-wind-trajectory RMSE falls by 11.67% at T11
and 24.45% at T07; paired seven-day intervals are 4.91–21.43% and 16.78–41.87%.
SMARTEOLE's corresponding reduction is 5.81%, with an interval including zero.
Across all three reference populations, internal-geometry errors fall by
9.58–26.94%, with positive seven-day intervals.

Common-duration contrasts, temporal-order controls, all-population outcomes and
complete validation/model comparisons retain the scientific context of this
complexity-dependent benefit. T11 and T07 share one farm; SMARTEOLE supplies a
second farm. Each test population occupies four seven-day blocks. The task uses
complete event context to reconstruct measured processes retrospectively.
