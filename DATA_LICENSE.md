# Provider-authorized data license

The data provider explicitly confirmed open redistribution and MIT licensing for the provider-contributed Chinese wind-farm SCADA and researcher-produced annotation material on 15 September 2026.

For those contributions, the MIT terms in `LICENSE` apply. The v0.2.0 release manifest identifies the Chinese raw inputs by their archive paths and checksums. Farm and turbine identifiers are retained for scientific correspondence; participant names and contact details are not requested by the v15 annotation interface.

This notice does not replace the licenses of externally acquired ERA5, NOAA, SDWPF, Greek, La Haute Borne or Hill of Towie data. Their original provider licenses and attribution requirements remain applicable.
