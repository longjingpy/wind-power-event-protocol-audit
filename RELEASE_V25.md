# v25 evidence included in the v0.7.0 / manuscript-v26 release

The v25 evidence is integrated into `AppliedEnergy_current_v26.zip`.
The economic replication asset remains available in v0.6.0.

## New evidence

- Eight populations and thirteen common-budget feature arms isolate scalar descriptors, five shared angular coordinates and a protected sign.
- Paired 3/7/14-day blocks report AUROC, log-loss and Brier differences.
- Dimension, pre-PCA signed-channel, random-sign, consistent-reversal, test-corruption, alternative-anchor and representation-noise checks accompany the primary contrasts.
- Seven actual AI review sessions assessed the existing 320 windows. The three human observers remain separate. Model outputs are not human survey responses.
- The main paper distinguishes encoding recovery from conditional increments, binds structural agreement to matched/encodable events, and reports four occupied LiDAR blocks per instrument.

`results/protocol_benchmark_v25/` contains aggregate physical tables, AI inputs and responses, source-separated agreement tables, and anonymous window-level class counts. Original individual human submissions and internal-clock physical predictions remain outside this public snapshot.

Run `python script/reproduce_panel_alpha_v25.py` to reproduce the six point alphas without individual human-response records. Full physical experiments require the study archives and previously fitted source transforms; this aggregate release is not a self-contained raw-data reproduction for every private-clock experiment.

Protected polarity restores information erased by angular encoding; extra value beyond scalar information varies with population and task. AI sessions share an inherited runtime/rubric; the effective model version was not exposed by the collaboration interface. No claim of seven different model families or additional human recruitment is made. All v24 economic outcomes are unchanged.
